# FoodOrderV1 v1.10.1

Firebase deploy quota fix and change-scoped function deploys

## Highlights

- **Firebase deploys were failing on arithmetic, not luck.** The CPU quota
  counts `cpu x maxInstances` across every deployed service, and `memory:
  '256MiB'` does not buy a fractional CPU: firebase-tools maps every memory
  tier at or below 1GiB to a full vCPU. Fifty functions therefore reserved
  fifty vCPU against a twenty vCPU cap, which is why the deploy failed on
  whichever batch happened to tip the region over, and why halving
  `maxInstances` earlier helped without ever being enough. `cpu` is now set
  explicitly at 0.167, putting the rollout peak at 9.7 of 20.
- **A change now deploys only the functions that depend on it.** Deploy scope
  comes from the real import graph rather than a hand-kept list of three
  isolated files that made a full thirty-five-minute deploy the common case.
  A leaf module deploys 5 functions instead of 50; a frontend or docs release
  deploys none. Aliased re-exports are mapped under their deployed names, and
  the deploy re-checks that the graph covers every exported function, falling
  back to a full deploy rather than risk silently skipping one.
- Rules 22 to 27 and the new deploy skill are reachable from their indexes;
  five of them had been orphaned.
