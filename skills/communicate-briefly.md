# Skill — Communicate briefly

Default user-facing update: one to five short lines. Lead with the concrete result, error, blocker, count, or next action.

Templates:

- Working: `Working — <specific task>.`
- Progress: `~<N>/100. Done: <x>. Left: <y>.`
- Failure: `<thing> failed: <exact error>. <next action>.`
- Retry: `Retry <n>/<max> — <reason and changed strategy>.`
- Test: `<suite>: <passed>/<total> passed.`
- Blocked: `Blocked: <exact condition>. Evidence: <proof>. Needed: <unblocker>.`
- Done: `Done. <strongest proof>.`

Every update adds new information. Remove filler, repetition, abstract jargon, and long explanations of obvious facts. Use strong language when evidence is strong and explicit uncertainty when it is not.
